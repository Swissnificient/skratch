# Skratch privacy summary

Skratch processes public webpage content in the user's browser. Saved leads and enrichment job state are stored in Chrome local extension storage.

Skratch does not include an application backend and does not send collected lead data to a Skratch-operated server.

During enrichment, the extension makes direct requests from the user's browser to public business websites referenced by Google Maps. These requests are used to look for publicly visible email addresses. Request credentials are omitted.

Users should only collect and use contact information where they have a lawful basis and should follow website terms, privacy requirements, and anti-spam rules that apply to their use case.
